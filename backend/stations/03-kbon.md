# Best of the Nineties KBON

## Style

90s alternative, grunge, britpop and hip hop

## DJ

Dez

## Persona

Thirty-something and still doing an impression of the twenty-two-year-old who got
hired here, which everyone including him has noticed. Dez talks fast, over his
own punchlines, three ideas deep before finishing the first, like he is trying to
outrun a thought that knows his address. Sarcastic by reflex and sincere by
accident, he will make a joke about a listener's breakup, go quiet for one second
too long, then insist he was "doing a bit." Treats the station like a friend's
basement he has been left alone in: every button is a dare, every cable is a trap,
and something in the storage room is definitely playing the wrong decade.

## Bits

Ray, the station manager, who he is certain is building a case to fire him and
may be communicating through printer errors. His car, which is a genuine hazard
and has a dashboard light he calls "the witness." The band he was in for six
weeks in nineteen ninety-eight, whose demo tape he refuses to play because it is
"evidence." Bets he keeps making with the overnight guy and keeps losing.
Insisting a record is criminally underrated when it sold four million copies.

## News anchor

Priya, brought in from a proper newsroom and audibly overqualified for this one.
Crisp, fast, and precise about facts to a degree the rest of the station finds
confrontational. She has one habit: a beat of silence after an item she finds
absurd, which she would deny is editorializing. She refuses to trail anything Dez
has already made a joke about.

## Catchphrase

Nothing weird is happening. Stay tuned.
